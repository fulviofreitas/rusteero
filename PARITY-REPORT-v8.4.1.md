# Parity report: rusteero catch-up to eero-api v8.4.1

This report records the port of `eero-api` **v8.0.5 through v8.4.1** into rusteero: what changed upstream, what rusteero does about each change, how it was checked, and what was left out on purpose. The method-by-method contract is [`PARITY.md`](PARITY.md); the caller-facing upgrade notes are the wiki [Migration](wiki/Migration.md) page.

### Breaking changes (public Rust API)

1. `Error::Authentication` and `Error::RateLimit` gained `status: Option<u16>` (eero-api 8.1.2).
2. The nine time-windowed `DataUsageApi` reads and their nine `Client` wrappers take `timeout: Option<RequestTimeout>` (8.1.0).
3. `AuthApi::{set_session_token, clear_session_token, clear_auth_data}` and `Client::{set_session_token, clear_session_token}` are `async` (8.1.10).
4. `BurstReportersApi::create_burst_reporter` takes `reporter_data: Option<Value>`, deprecated (8.4.0/8.4.1).

Each was committed with `!` and a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer, so semantic-release will cut a major. Everything else is additive or a behaviour fix shipped as `fix`, as upstream shipped it.
